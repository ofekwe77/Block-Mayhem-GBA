## Design principles (PART 44)

| Principle | How the build honours it | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Precision before spectacle | effects never block input; hitstop bounded to 8 frames; cosmetic events drained over frames | `ui.txt` hitstop_max, TESTING.md performance |
| Readability before complexity | special symbols keep their colours under every theme, CONTRAST theme, telegraph marks, HUD layouts without overlap | theme_*.png, stage screenshots |
| Skill before randomness | seeded 7-bag, special guarantees, skilled-paced bots clear far more than casual ones | BALANCE.md calibration |
| Powers create decisions | heat costs, cooldowns, targeting; no ability is a free shortcut | `abilities.*`, ability efficiency table |
| Surprises are learnable | every hazard and attack telegraphed; secret events are cosmetic only | boss audits, INTERACTIONS.md |
| Each world introduces something | ice, fire / lava, sparks / relay, gravity, everything combined | WORLD_PRODUCTION_MATRIX.md, STAGE_PASSPORTS.md |
| Bosses test mastery | later phases combine learned attacks | BOSS_PRODUCTION_MATRIX.md |
| Replayability from systems | Chaos Run builds, challenge modifiers, ranks | `run.*`, `challenges.*` |
| Hardware-aware | IWRAM renderer, palette animation, sprite / palette budgets | TESTING.md performance |
| Complete the real game | a working ROM with every mode | this document |

## Machine-verified vs human verification (PART 63.3)

Machine-verified: compilation, ROM validity, asset formats and integration, every rule and interaction listed
in INTERACTIONS.md, all 30 stages, all 6 boss encounters, every mode's main loop, saves, audio playback,
performance, documentation consistency.

## Needs human judgement (not claimed)

- **Fun and feel** (META-06): pacing, satisfaction of combos and boss fights. Proxies only: bot balance runs,
  fairness checks, screenshot reviews.
- **Musical quality** (AUD-06): the 14 tracks are original and verified to play and loop; whether they are
  good music needs a listener.
- **Animation appeal and visual taste**: reviewed on screenshots by the developer, not by players.

## Known limitations

- Balance is calibrated with human-paced bots, not with players; the dual-board bosses are the hardest fights
  for bots and may be for people.
- Verified in the mGBA core through a headless harness; not tested on physical hardware or a flash cart.
- The diagnostic mailbox (`core/test_interface.h`) remains in the ROM as a test path; it cannot be reached
  from the game's interface.
