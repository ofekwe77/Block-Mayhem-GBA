# Balance values

All numbers live in `core/bm_config.h` (single source of truth); stage and boss content lives in
`core/bm_stages.cpp` / `core/bm_bosses.cpp`. This file explains the values and how they were checked.
Simulation evidence: `tests/build/balance_report.md`, `tests/build/ability_report.md`,
`tests/build/stage_report.md` and `tests/build/run_report.md` (regenerated by `.\build.ps1 tests`).

## Overheat
| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| Trigger | heat reaches 100 (meter consumed to 0) |
| Duration | 720 frames of active play (paused by pause, resolution animations, boss transitions) |
| Success | clear 2 rows during the window |
| Failure | garbage rows: Beginner 1, Standard 2, Expert 2 with split gaps; warning shown in last 3 s |

## Special cells
Bomb 3×3 (power 1); Fire range 1 (4 neighbours); Spark range 4 (cap 6 with upgrades); Ice 1 hp;
Armor 2 hp; Magnet 2 columns ±1 row; Rainbow 2 targets; max 32 waves per resolution (safety).
Distribution: one special piece every 5–7 locks (normal), 3–5 (advanced); rainbow weight 4 vs bomb 40.

## Dual board
Forced switch after 4 locks; CROSS OVER = rows on both boards within 4 locks (+1000 × level, +15 heat);
2+ rows on one board: +6 heat and one row removed from the other board's pending garbage;
4 rows: cancels up to 2 pending garbage rows on the other board.

## Chaos Arcade events (PART 22.2)

Endless, no objectives. A telegraphed environmental event (3 locks of warning) rotates through garbage
drip, freeze cells, surge, molten rain, armor plates and a left/right gravity shift. The first event comes
16 locks in; every level brings events one lock sooner, down to 6 locks apart (`arcade_event_interval`,
`arcade_event_min_interval`). Shield Matrix blocks an event like any hazard.

## Challenge modifiers (PART 27.3, 30.7 — `core/bm_config.h` `challenge_*`)
| Challenge | Score multiplier | Rule values |
|---|---|---|
| TURBO | 125% | start level +4 |
| NO HOLD | 115% | hold disabled |
| ONE NEXT | 110% | one preview |
| FRENZY | 80% | special interval halved (arcade 5-7 -> 2-3 pieces) |
| RISING TIDE | 130% | 1 junk row every 1200 frames of play, telegraphed 2 locks ahead, shield cannot stop it |
| SHADOW STACK | 150% | presentation only |

Multipliers multiply (all six: x2.43) and apply to every score event. FRENZY pays less because specials
make big scores easier: measured 19 -> 45 special pieces per 100 pieces (test challenges.frenzy_doubles_special_pieces).
The presentation-only challenges change nothing else: the same seed plays the same game and the score is
exactly the scaled sum of the score events (test challenges.presentation_challenges_only_scale_the_score).

## Stage ranks (PART 27.2)
Points: score >= S target 3 (>= 2/3: 2, >= 1/3: 1); time <= par 2 (<= 1.5 x par: 1); no garbage taken 1;
bonus goal 2. Rank: 8 = S+, 6-7 = S, 4-5 = A, 2-3 = B, else C. Every criterion is shown on the stage card or
the results screen, and none is impossible: each stage's par and S target come from the calibration below.

## Calibration with human-paced bots (SIM-03)
`tests/test_balance.cpp` plays every stage with the resource-aware bot slowed down to human speed:
*skilled* = 14-21 frames of reaction per piece, one button tap every 4 frames, 4% of pieces placed one
column off, on STANDARD (4 seeds); *casual* = 26-39 frames, a tap every 7 frames, 12% misplaced, on
BEGINNER (3 seeds). Results of the current tuning: skilled 108/120 clears (World 1: 18/20),
casual 62/90 (Worlds 1-2: 22/30). The test fails if World 1 drops below 90% for skilled players,
Worlds 1-2 below 60% for casual players, or the campaign below 70% for skilled players.

- **Par time** = 2.5 x the skilled clear time, rounded up to 10 s (the skilled bot is a fast player, about
  1.7 pieces per second; par asks for roughly 0.7).
- **S target** = 0.85 x the skilled score, rounded to 500.
- **Time-limit bonus goals** = 2 x the skilled clear time (a stretch goal).
- Tuning made from the data: 4-2 MAGNETIC STORM (16 rows + 3 magnets at level 6 took three times longer than
  the stages around it and casual players never finished) -> 14 rows + 2 magnets at level 5; 5-1 SIDEWAYS
  (the World 5 opener lost half of the skilled runs) -> level 5, 12 rows, settle shifts every 9 locks, fewer
  bombs; THE BLOCK MONSTER (first boss, casual 1/3) -> 8 / 7 / 6 locks between attacks instead of 7 / 6 / 5.

## Bosses: HP against damage (BAL-04)
| Boss | HP | Skilled fight | Damage / min | Typical player (2.5x) | Casual wins (Beginner) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Block Monster | 100 | 34 s | 176 | ~1.4 min | 2/3 |
| Cryo Titan | 140 | 49 s | 171 | ~2.0 min | 2/3 |
| Magma Hydra | 180 | 58 s | 186 | ~2.4 min | 2/3 |
| Electric Emperor | 220 | 62 s | 213 | ~2.6 min | 1/3 |
| Gravity King | 260 | 83 s | 188 | ~3.5 min | 2/3 |
| Core of Mayhem | 350 | 95 s | 221 | ~4.0 min | 1/3 |

Damage per minute stays in a narrow 170-220 band, so HP alone sets the fight length: fights grow from about
a minute and a half to four minutes, with the two dual-board bosses as the hardest. Win rate over 24 seeded fights at the skilled pace (2026-10-17; the boss verification tests
`boss1..6.*_full_verification` repeat a smaller sample on every test run): Electric Emperor 16/24, Gravity King 23/24, Core of Mayhem 14/24. The Emperor was
measured at 15/24 with phases 2-3 attacking every 5 locks from level 6, i.e. as hard as the final boss: its
later phases now attack every 6 locks and the fight starts at level 5 (cap 6) like the 4-3 relay stage, so the
final boss stays the hardest fight. The dual-board fights remain the hardest for the bot because a relay loss
is a top-out of one narrow 8x16 board.

The boss audit (`tests/test_bosses.cpp`) also found that combination attacks did not prepare their parts:
a core combo of molten rain + fire breath landed no blocks and ice / armour combos marked no cells. Combos now
prepare and telegraph both parts (Magma Hydra phase 3, Gravity King phase 4, Core of Mayhem phase 5), which
made those fights slightly harder; the S targets of 3-5, 4-5, 5-5 and 6-5 were recalibrated afterwards
(42 500, 99 500, 61 500, 121 000).

No boss can be burst:
`exploit.boss_damage_never_exceeds_the_per_resolution_cap` plays every boss with the strongest Chaos Run
build and Overheat; the biggest single resolution reached 53 of a 72 cap and every kill took 10+ hits.

## Ability efficiency (BAL-03)
Chaos Arcade, skilled pace, 3 seeds x 3 minutes, one ability equipped (`tests/build/ability_report.md`):

| Ability | Uses / run | Score vs no ability | Reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Gravity Pulse | 1.3 | +35% | strongest board reset; 45 heat + 5-lock cooldown keep it occasional |
| Nuclear Burst | 1.0 | +25% | one big swing per run at 80 heat |
| Emergency Vent | 0.3 | -3% | a pure survival tool (no score by design) |
| Time Freeze | 0.3 | -6% | precision tool; matters under pressure, not for raw score |
| Chain Spark | 3.7 | -12% | competes with Overheat for heat |
| Laser Beam | 4.3 | -19% | frequent cheap fixes; spends the heat that would fuel Overheat |
| Shield Matrix | 10 | -20% | the bot shields every event; worth it against real attacks, not chip events |
| Shape Shift | - | - | needs human judgement (not modelled by the bot) |

Reading: spending heat on abilities trades Overheat score for board control, which is the intended tension
(PART 8). No ability is mandatory or dominant: the best gain is +35% and the most expensive tools are the
ones with the biggest effect.

## Exploit checks (SIM-04)
`tests/test_exploits.cpp`: input spam (move / rotate / hold / select without dropping) scores nothing;
special-effect points stay under the 500 cap even for a full 5x5 Nuclear Burst; the boss damage cap holds
for every boss against a maxed build; switching dual boards alone yields no combo, heat or score; heat,
Overheat counts, score monotonicity and cooldowns stay valid through 12 minutes of heavy bot play;
SECOND CHANCE is a single rescue per run. Achievements need real events (`test_achievements.cpp`) and
tampered saves are rejected (`test_save.cpp`).
