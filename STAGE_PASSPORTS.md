# BLOCK MAYHEM — Stage Design Passports (PART 50.3)

Generated by `tools/gen_docs.cpp` (`tools/bm.sh docs`) from the stage, boss and balance data the ROM
uses (`core/bm_stages.cpp`, `core/bm_bosses.cpp`, `core/bm_config.h`). The fairness figures are fresh
simulations of each stage with the real session code: eight seeded runs by the resource-aware test
bot (instant inputs) and one idle run. Human-paced calibration (reaction time, input delay and
misplacements) is in BALANCE.md.

Common to every stage — **randomization policy:** pieces come from a seeded 7-bag; special blocks are
decorated onto pieces by a separate seeded distributor (interval and weights below, with guarantees);
hazard and boss targets (columns, cells, gaps) use their own seeded streams, so presentation effects
can never change them (PART 55.6). **Failure criteria:** a piece cannot spawn (top out) or incoming
garbage pushes the stack out of the well. **Automated tests:** host `stages.all_thirty_stages_have_a_route_to_completion`
(12 bot runs, invariants checked every frame), `stages.every_stage_can_be_lost_and_idling_never_wins`,
`balance.human_paced_stage_report`; ROM `tests/emu/stages_wN.txt` (loads, plays to victory with the
autoplay bot, results and progression, screenshot `stage_W_I.png`).
